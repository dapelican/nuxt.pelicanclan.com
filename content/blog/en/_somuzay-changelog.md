# SoMuZay changelog

## 2023-06-13

- I changed the positionning of SoMuZay, as I felt it was way too generic. I changed the value proposition from "Random music videos by vibes" to "Rap and R&B music videos".
- I also made the new page "Hits" visible. It is now the homepage of the website.
- On the advanced search page, I made all vibes visible in the filters.

## 2023-01

### SEO

This month, I replaced the plugin I was using to display social share buttons ([AddToAny Share Buttons](https://www.addtoany.com/buttons/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}) by a much lighter one: [Novashare](https://novashare.io/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}. Novashare is developed by the same team behind [Perfmattters](https://perfmatters.io/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}, a performance plugin I am already using to improve drastically the speed of the website.

The result is incredible. The music videos pages are above the score of 90 on both mobile and desktop on [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}. In terms of speed and performance, I feel like I've done everything possible within my budget and accordingly to the current number of visits. The next major improvement would be to upgrade the hosting plan of the website, which is still the most basic one of my hosting company, [Infomaniak](http://?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}.

I'm really satisfied with the current results because it took me several months to get there. I had to find the right plugins to use in WordPress. There were some trials and errors made in the process. I feel SoMuZay has a solid foundation to get good rankings on search engines.

### Design and functionality

On December 7th, I added a SweetPotion button at the bottom of every SweetPotion music video. This button redirects to a random music video. This new button makes it easier for visitors to go the next video. It's so simple, but I had to have the right plugin and theme to do it easily. Specials thanks to [GeneratePress](https://generatepress.com/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"} for that. As a consequence, I got rid of the page "How it works" that I implemented in October 2022.

On December 9th, I also modified the links in the footer so that they can appear on a single line instead of several ones. Before, there were on dispatched on several columns and lines. They took way too much space. I was trying to use the menu functionality of WordPress. But I ended up adding some custom HTML and CSS for the links. It turns out to be much better.

On December 24th, I tried to fix somehow the problem of the search on SoMuZay. So far, the search experience on SoMuZay has been terrible. This is probably the weakest point of WordPress. In particular:

1. You must exactly type what you're looking for to find it. No typos are allowed.
2. It's not possible to have the artists pages (tag pages) in the search results.
3. There is no autocompletion of the results.

I haven't found yet a satisfying solution in terms of functionnality and budget. For now, I'm still investigating the topic and I opted for a temporary solution. I added a page "Search (Beta)" that links a special Google page to search through the website. I'm trying [Google's Programmable Search Engine](https://programmablesearchengine.google.com/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}. There are still some limitations to this solution:

1. The custom search engine only searches through the pages Google has indexed. Many pages of SoMuZay are still not indexed by Google, so the results are clearly incomplete.
2. The custom search engine only allows autocompletion of popular searches made on Google. So, most suggestions are irrelevant when applied to SoMuZay.
3. The custom search engine shows ads just like Google does. Except that they are much more irrelevant. There is a paying option to get rid of the ads: $5 per 1000 requests. I'l see later on if this is adequate.

## 2022-11

### Video sitemap

### New page created

On November 26th, I created a new page called "Newly added". This page shows the latest music videos and music projects I added to the website. I also added on the home page this sentence: "New music videos added every day". This clairifes how often the website is updated, and also put some pressure on me to indeed update it every day.

## 2022-10

### How it works

I provided a visual explanation about how to watch random music videos. There is a sentence on the homepage: *Each click on "SweetPotion" redirects you to a random Rap/R&B music video*. I was worry that this sentence wasn't as obvious to visitors as it was for me. I made a video, transformed it into a gif and created the [How it works](https://www.somuzay.com/how-it-works?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"} page. I recorded the screen, with no sound, thanks to the Chrome extension [Awesome screenshot](https://www.awesomescreenshot.com/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}. I also used a mouse highlighter extension to make the mouse movements more visible during the video.

### Footer

The footer was quite a mess. It was taking too much place. So I put all the links on a single line and deleted the vertical blocks that were there before.

### Content

Growing SoMuZay is a numbers game. The more videos I add on the site, the more chances it gets to be ranked on search engines, and the more pleasant it is for visitors to watch random videos. This virtuous circle hopefully brings more visitors without advertising.

My main focus is to add music videos, as much as I can, without compromising the quality of the information. For each video, I look for the video director and the music producer. I try to add at least several music videos per day (after my day at work). On the weekends, I try to add more.

For now, I'm adding 2 videos per artist. I have a huge Excel file, that have been maintaining for more than 12 years. The artists are ordered by name in alphabetical order (A to Z). I select an artist, prepare the music videos to be created on the website, and move to the next artist. The last music videos I added in October were the 2 first music videos of the R&B artist [Chrisette Michelle](https://www.somuzay.com/as/?_artist=chrisette+michele&utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}. The first ones that I will add in November are from Christina Milian.

As of November 1st, there are **223 random music videos**.

### Peak of September 27th

On September 27th, there was a peak of visitors that I can explain.

I recently focused on adding rap and r&b releases on SoMuZay. But I also added before that many releases from artists from the French West Indies.

In particular, I had added all the music videos and music projects of a rapper named [Dawa Star](https://www.somuzay.com/as/?_artist=dawa+star&utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"} He is from the French Island of Guadeloupe, in the Caribbean sea. He mostly released mixtapes.

He passed away on September 26th. Many visits of this day and the day after were people seaching for his mixtapes. Since SoMuZay is the only website having all of his releases, the site benefited from good positions on Google.

### New theme

Another major change was done on SoMuZay on September 30th. I was unhappy with the theme I had been using so far: the Twenty Nineteen theme. This theme is a free native theme from Wordpress, but it is quite slow: I was consistently below 90 on [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"} on mobiles. I was also bothered by the lack the flexibility of the theme, and I wanted to redesign the order of elements of on some pages, without having to mess with the code of the theme.

I had been looking for a simple but very flexible WordPress theme for months. But on this friday, right after my day of work, instead of looking for "simple WordPress theme" or any other similar query, I looked for "Fast WordPress theme". And I took the time to explore.

I arrived on this page: [16 Fastest WordPress Themes in 2022 (Based on Thorough Testing)](https://kinsta.com/blog/fastest-wordpress-theme/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}. And a miracle happened. I discovered [GeneratePress](https://generatepress.com/?utm_source=pelicanclan.com&utm_medium=article){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}. A fast theme, but also a fairly easy and simple one. On that same evening, I bought the lifetime package of the theme ($249). I customized everything to finally deliver the experience the visitors deserve. I started with the research on Google at 6pm and I finished 8 hours later, at 2am.

This moment was incredible to live. I felt alive, excited, and I was ready to go for hours. At 2 am, I was still feeling fresh. I was happy of the major change I had just made, on the spot, without second thoughts. I was very pleased with the new version of SoMuzay.

The website is now quite fast, and every music project page has the right structure. This is another groundbreaking improvement for SoMuzay. A fast website makes the difference in SEO. I expect to see the results of this in the next months, with better rankings of SoMuZay on search engines. We'll see in the next reports.

## 2022-08

### Analytics

Compared to July 2022:

- The number of visitors is stable: +1%.
- The number of pageviews is decreasing: -11%. I was expecting an increase with the new feature I implemented on the website in July (see next section).
- The duration of visits is skyrocketing: +48%. This is probably due the fact that visitors tend to watch longer the music video they stumble on.

### New feature

This month of August is the first month with the new feature of SoMuZay: suggest random rap and R&B music videos from the homepage.

For several months, I had been stuck with a major problem: I didn't know what to feature on the homepage. I couldn't feature the most recent releases, like the majority of music other blogs do, since I wasn't tracking these. I clearly cannot win at this game. I don't want to participate either. I wanted to have a different positioning. My shit should be different.

So I was focusing on adding releases from artists mainly active in the 1990s and the 2000s. But I couldn't directly feature these releases on the homepage, for 2 reasons:

1. It wouldn't make much sense. A new visitor discovering the website would feel like landing on an outdated website. He would hardly come back, since he would naturally assume that the website is not updated anymore.
2. Even if I wanted to, I couldn't do this easily from a technical standpoint. I would have to tag and untag posts overtime.

I had to solve this issue: how to keep adding older releases while still making the website look alive and regularly updated?

In July, I had the idea of showcasing random music videos. For me, this feature is a game changer and it is the best of both world:

1. I'm still able to add releases from any era.
2. Visitors can come to the website and get unseen content. They don't have the wrong impression that nothing happens here.

I added a sentence on the homepage to explain how it works: *Each click on "SweetPotion" redirects you to a random Rap/R&B music video*. I hope that visitors will understand it, but I feel that there is something missing. I'll keep brainstorming to find something better.
