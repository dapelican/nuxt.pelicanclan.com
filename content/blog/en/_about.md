
# About me

## Who Am I?

Hi, I'm Dapelican. I'm a JavaScript web developer based in Paris, France.

Some facts about me:
- I was born and I lived the first 18 years of my life in
  [Guadeloupe](https://goo.gl/maps/fWWaZzyMj9Kexm6m9){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"},
  a French Caribbean archipelago.
  A very beautiful place.
- My MBTI profile is ISTJ.
  I'm a [logistician](https://www.16personalities.com/istj-personality){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}.
- I love talking to anyone, in a one-to-one context.
  But I avoid meeting people in groups.
  I hate the group thing because:
  - The conversations are way too superficial.
  - People behave differently when they are in group.
- I worked 10 years in the marketing field before becoming a web developer in my mid thirties.
  A couple of years ago, I was an "old" junior developer.
- I knew nothing in IT.
  I had to learn everything from scratch.
  I learnt the fundamentals at [Launch School](https://launchschool.com/){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-primary"}.
  I started at 33.
  I got my first job in a SAAS company at 36.
